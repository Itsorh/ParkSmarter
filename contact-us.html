Contact Us

Before you contact your local council please consider if the issue is one of obstruction or dangerous parking.

Obstruction or dangerous parking

If the issue is one of obstruction or dangerous parking and there are no parking restrictions in place (commonly denoted by lines and signs) please contact the police.

The police retain responsibility for all offences which are not civil offences and where penalty points are awarded against a motorist.

All moving traffic offences, such as contravening a one-way system or access only restriction, are also the responsibility of the police.

Read more on the Derbyshire Police website or telephone: 101.

On street parking enforcement

For enquiries relating to on street parking enforcement in:

  • Countryside parks
  • Peak District National Park (within Derbyshire)
  • North East Derbyshire
  • South Derbyshire

Contact Derbyshire County Council, email: contact.centre@derbyshire.gov.uk or telephone: 01629 533190.

General enquiries

Enquiries of a general nature (other than enforcement) should be directed to the relevant local council:

  • North East Derbyshire District Council
  • South Derbyshire District Council
  • Peak District National Park

For all other boroughs and districts please contact:

  • Amber Valley Borough Council - 01773 570222 or email: parking@ambervalley.gov.uk
  • Chesterfield Borough Council - 01246 345593 or email: parkingservices@chesterfield.gov.uk
  • Derbyshire Dales District Council - 01629 761100 or email: carparks@derbyshiredales.gov.uk
  • Erewash Borough Council - 0115 9072244 or email: enquiries@erewash.gov.uk
  • High Peak Borough Council - 0845 129 7777 or email: carparks@highpeak.gov.uk

Local authorities have no involvement in the enforcement of:

  • privately owned car parks
  • charitable trust car parks
  • sites operated by other agencies (such as Severn Trent Water).
