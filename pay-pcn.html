Pay PCN

You can pay your PCN online, by post or by telephone.

Pay online

You can pay your PCN online using the Park Smarter automated online payment system.

You may use the system to pay any outstanding PCNs using your credit or debit card.

We accept Visa, MasterCard, Switch, Maestro, Solo or Delta.

You will need your PCN number and your vehicle registration to hand.

Submit (opens in new window)

By telephone

We accept credit and debit card payments if you call our automated payment line 0345 520 2468.

You can call 24 hours a day, 7 days a week.

Please ensure you have your card details and PCN to hand.

By post

Cheques and postal orders can be made payable to Park Smarter.

Please write your PCN number and your vehicle registration number on the back of the cheque.

Send your payment together with the payment slip to:

Central Processing Unit
PO 10282
Sutton in Ashfield
NG17 0DX

Please allow 2 working days for first class post and 5 working days for second class post.
