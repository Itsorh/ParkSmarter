Cookies

This Cookies Policy explains what cookies are, how we use them on our website, and the choices you have regarding their use. We aim to be transparent about how your data is used so you can feel confident while browsing our site.

What are cookies

Cookies are small text files stored on your device when you visit a website. They help websites function properly, improve performance, and provide insights into how visitors use the site. Cookies do not harm your device and cannot be used to run programs or deliver viruses.

How we use cookies

We use cookies to:

  • Ensure the website works correctly
  • Improve site performance and user experience
  • Understand how visitors use our pages
  • Remember your preferences
  • Provide relevant content or features

We do not use cookies to collect sensitive personal information.

Types of cookies we use

Essential cookies

These cookies are required for the website to function. They enable features such as page navigation, security, and access to protected areas. You cannot disable these cookies.

Performance and analytics cookies

These cookies help us understand how visitors interact with our website by collecting anonymous data. This allows us to improve content, layout, and functionality.

Functionality cookies

These cookies remember your preferences (such as language or region) to provide a more personalised experience.

Advertising or targeting cookies

These cookies may be set by us or third‑party partners to deliver relevant ads or track performance across websites.

Examples of cookies we use

Here are some examples of cookies that may be used on our website:

  • session_id — Maintains your session while browsing (expires when you close your browser)
  • _ga — Helps us understand site usage through analytics (expires after 2 years)
  • _gid — Distinguishes users for analytics (expires after 24 hours)

If we use additional tools such as YouTube embeds, Facebook Pixel, or Hotjar, they may set their own cookies. Their policies apply to those cookies.

Third‑party cookies

Some cookies are placed by third‑party services we use, such as analytics providers, embedded videos or maps, social media integrations, or advertising networks. These third parties may use their own cookies to track usage across websites. We recommend reviewing their privacy policies for more details.

Managing your cookie preferences

Using our cookie tools

If our website provides a cookie banner or preferences panel, you can use it to accept, reject, or adjust non‑essential cookies at any time.

Using your browser settings

Most web browsers allow you to control cookies through their settings, including blocking or deleting cookies, clearing browsing data, or setting rules for specific websites. Please note that disabling certain cookies may affect how the website functions.

Legal basis for using cookies

We rely on legitimate interest for essential cookies that are required for the website to operate, and on your consent for all non‑essential cookies such as analytics, functionality, and advertising cookies. You can withdraw your consent at any time by adjusting your cookie settings.

Changes to this policy

We may update this Cookies Policy from time to time to reflect changes in technology, legislation, or our practices. Any updates will be posted on this page with a revised “Last updated” date.
